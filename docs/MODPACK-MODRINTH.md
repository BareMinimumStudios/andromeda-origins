**A medieval fantasy world. A story shaped by the people who call it home.**

Build a village beneath unfamiliar stars. Carry a letter across a rival's border. Open a shop, swear an oath, or uncover something your character was never meant to find.

Andromeda is a **16+ roleplay and civilization modpack** built for the Andromeda community. Mythology, celestial themes, and hidden lore set the scene; your characters, settlements, and choices give it a history.

## Find your place

**Become someone.** Choose a fantasy origin with its own abilities, strengths, and limitations. Give your character ambitions, loyalties, and a reason to step beyond their front door. [Andromeda Origins](https://modrinth.com/mod/andromeda-origins) brings the community's species into the game.

**Build something that lasts.** Found a settlement, join an established town, or help a community grow into a nation. Land claiming protects the places you build while politics, cooperation, and conflict shape what they become.

**Keep the world connected.** Work as a courier, deliver mail, establish trade routes, or build a business around what another settlement needs. A journey can carry goods, news, or the beginning of your next plot.

**Follow what lies beyond.** Explore a world with lore to discover and room for stories of your own. A quiet encounter, a community event, or a discovery far from home can change the direction of your character's life.

## Your story belongs here

Andromeda is for players who enjoy **character-driven roleplay, settlement building, trade, and long-term worldbuilding**. You do not need to lead a nation to matter: a craftsperson, traveler, courier, or neighbor can leave just as lasting a mark.

The pack is designed around the **Andromeda multiplayer community and server experience**. Server rules, applications, events, and player interaction are part of that experience; installing the pack is the first step toward joining it.

## Before you arrive

Our community is **16+**. Read the server rules, joining and application details, guides, and origin information before creating your character.
